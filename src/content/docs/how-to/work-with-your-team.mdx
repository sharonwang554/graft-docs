Learnings reach your teammates through git, with no server.

## Share learnings

Commit `.trail/`, and every clone has them. Learnings on a teammate's pushed branch show up before it merges, marked "pushed and not merged yet", with only their first lines.

## See what the team learned this week

```bash
trail team
```

`trail team` shows the last 7 days in your terminal: what the team learned, who's working on what, and where work overlaps.

## Find out when a teammate changes the same code

In Claude Code, your agent says so right after it edits code a teammate's open branch also changes, and whether the two clash. See [Integrate with Claude Code](/how-to/integrate-claude-code/#what-a-session-gets).
